# Lesson 3 Homework: Policy and Evidence Map

**Suggested preparation for:** Lesson 4

## Study

- Review the secure development and compliance objectives in the [current GH-100 study guide](https://aka.ms/GH100-StudyGuide).
- Focus on enterprise policies, security posture, data protection, audit logging, and reporting.
- Use the linked exam resources and documentation from the study guide; note any version- or plan-dependent detail to verify rather than memorize as universal.

## Practice

1. Open the [lesson guide](lesson-guide.md) and reuse the `web` branch and release-tag requirements.
2. Check your Step 2 control map: every row needs a target, rule, scope, owner, and source.
3. Check Step 3's test table. Explain an allowed and a blocked case; keep unrun results labelled "not run".
4. Revisit Riley's emergency request using Step 4. Check approval, narrow exception, retained evidence, and return to normal protection.
5. Add one example of a legitimate change your design might accidentally block. Explain which rule you would investigate, without weakening unrelated protections.

## Retrieval Practice

On three different days, explain one control from the map, its scope, and how you would prove it is working.

## Bring to Class

- The policy map and counterexample.
- One point where the setting alone is insufficient evidence.
