# Lesson 2 Homework: GitHub Enterprise Cloud Decision Brief

**Suggested preparation for:** Lesson 3

## Study

- Review the deployment and licensing objectives in the [current GH-100 study guide](https://aka.ms/GH100-StudyGuide).
- Study the deployment overview and licensing material in the official [GitHub Administration Part 1 learning path](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/github-administration-products/).

## Practice

1. Open the [lesson guide](lesson-guide.md). Choose Harbor, Cedar, or Summit from the supplied profiles.
2. Use Step 1's account comparison and Step 2's recommendation table to write a brief with four headings: known needs, missing facts, proposed account type, and reason.
3. Add one condition that could change your proposal and a supporting documentation link.
4. Revisit the billing checklist from Step 3. Name the usage report or agreement detail still needed for this company.
5. Revisit Avery's access issue from Step 4. Complete your draft ticket's missing-evidence list without inventing test results.

Do not invent prices, feature availability, or compliance guarantees. Mark those items as questions to verify against current documentation and the customer's actual plan.

## Retrieval Practice

On three different days, explain the tradeoffs between GHEC with personal accounts and GHEC with Enterprise Managed Users, then name two constraints that would make each a poor fit.

## Bring to Class

- The one-page brief.
- The three most important clarifying questions you would ask a stakeholder.
