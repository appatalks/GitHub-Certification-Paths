# Lesson 1 Homework: GitHub Model, Identity, and Access

**Suggested preparation for:** Lesson 2

## Study

- Read the audience profile and identity/access sections in the [current GH-100 study guide](https://aka.ms/GH100-StudyGuide).
- Complete or review [Manage user identities and GitHub authentication](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/modules/authenticate-authorize-user-identities-github/).
- Start the official [GitHub Administration Part 1 learning path](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/github-administration-products/). Focus on administration, access, and identity lifecycle.
- Use the [curated GitHub Administration path](https://github.com/appatalks/GitHub-Certification-Paths/blob/main/Resources/administration/README.md) for its boundary-design mission and self-check questions.

## Practice

1. Open the [lesson guide](lesson-guide.md) and check your sanitized `training-org` map against Step 1 and the [sandbox setup](../sandbox-setup.md). Use the Northstar table only for the separate paper exercise; do not publish real identities.
2. Revisit Step 2 and your two separate account-model diagrams. Label sign-in, account creation, and permission; do not combine them into a universal flow.
3. Revisit Step 3's access-change table. For each person, check that it includes required work, access to remove, an owner, and a verification plan.
4. Choose one unanswered question from Step 4. Look it up, add the source, and explain whether it changed your proposal.
5. Check the guide's completion checklist. Record anything you still cannot explain.

## Retrieval Practice

On separate occasions, explain account creation, sign-in, and permission without notes. Then compare with your two diagrams and correct any mixed-up terms. Repeat at your own pace until you can explain the distinction clearly.

## Bring to Class

- Your object map and access matrix.
- The joiner/mover/leaver checklist.
- Two identity decisions you want to discuss.

Use aliases for sandbox observations and fictional identities for paper scenarios. Follow the approved disposable-resource scope for any live test. Do not connect a real identity provider or use a production environment.
