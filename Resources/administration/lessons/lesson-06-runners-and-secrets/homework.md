# Lesson 6 Homework: Runner and Secret Boundaries

**Suggested preparation for:** Lesson 7

## Study

- Review the runner, networking, and encrypted-secrets objectives in the [current GH-100 study guide](https://aka.ms/GH100-StudyGuide).
- Revisit Actions policy and secrets topics that were difficult in the practice review.

## Practice

1. Open the [lesson guide](lesson-guide.md) and reuse its workload table, runner snapshot, and Q-1 record.
2. Revisit Step 1's recommendations. For each workload, name the requirement that most influenced your runner choice.
3. Rebuild the Step 2 match table without notes, then check it against the supplied labels, group access, and state.
4. Revisit Step 3's secret table. Explain an allowed and a denied consumer for each key without using a value.
5. Revisit Step 4. Name the measurement that would distinguish queue wait from slow execution before proposing more capacity.

## Retrieval Practice

On three different days, explain the runner decision for one workload, then identify what evidence would change it.

## Bring to Class

- The runner and secret-boundary brief.
- One runner recommendation you would change if a stated assumption proved false.
